About Ranju

Guidance rooted in the old texts, spoken in today's language

I began my journey in Jyotish through structured study and professional training in Vedic astrology and numerology in Jaipur. Over the years, I have read birth charts for families making decisions about marriage, career, health and property — always combining astrological insights with the practical questions people actually bring in with them.

What she offers

Four ways to find the guidance you’re looking for

Vedic Birth Chart Reading

A full reading of your Janam Kundli — planetary positions, current dasha period and the houses that matter most for the question you bring, whether that's timing, health or general direction.

Numerology Consultation

Your life path, expression and destiny numbers, drawn from your birth date and name — used to understand recurring patterns and to choose favourable dates for weddings, launches or travel.

Marriage Compatibility (Kundli Milan)

Guna Milan scoring across both charts, plus a plain-language walk-through of where the match is strong and where a bit of care will help — for families weighing a proposal.

Career & Business Astrology

A focused look at the second, sixth, tenth and eleventh houses to weigh a job change, a new venture, or the right time to make either move.

How a reading works

Four steps from booking to remedy

01

Share your details

Send your date, time and place of birth through the booking form — accuracy here matters more than anything else in the reading.

02

Chart preparation

Ranju casts your Kundli and reviews it before the session, so your appointment time is spent talking, not calculating.

03

The reading itself

A one-on-one conversation, in person or over video, working through your chart against the questions you came in with.

04

Notes and remedies

A written summary follows within three days, along with any suggested remedies — a gemstone, a mantra, or simply a date to keep in mind.
